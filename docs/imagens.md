# Imagens do projeto

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, em duas chamadas. Não foi utilizada API por CLI.

- `assets/images/logo-nova.png`: logo original fornecido pelo usuário, sem alterações.
- `assets/images/estrada-nova.webp`: fotografia ilustrativa da abertura e da opção carro. Original preservado em `assets/images/estrada-nova-original.png`.
- `assets/images/moto-nova.webp`: fotografia ilustrativa da opção moto. Original preservado em `assets/images/moto-nova-original.png`.

Os WebPs são cópias otimizadas, sem alterações na composição. As cenas foram geradas para esta landing page e não representam clientes, veículos ou locais verificados da empresa.

## Prompt da estrada

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium photorealistic wide landscape automotive campaign photograph for a Brazilian vehicle protection company named Nova Proteção. Image contains NO text, NO typography, NO logos, NO watermarks. Aspect ratio approximately 16:9, high resolution. A beautiful modern metallic graphite silver compact SUV, generic unbranded design, front three-quarter view facing LEFT, parked at a safe scenic coastal road turnout on the northeast coast of Brazil. The SUV dominates the RIGHT HALF of the frame, entire car visible, wheels realistic. A small tasteful black road motorcycle parked further behind the SUV, subtle, on far right. Camera low at headlight height. Broad winding paved coastal highway bends from foreground into distant green cliffs, blue Atlantic ocean visible in the upper right, subtle palms, big open sky. Late afternoon sunlight gives warm highlights on vehicle edges, sky is airy light blue rather than orange, ocean teal. Premium real editorial photography, 35mm lens, restrained cinematic color grade, natural textures, no oversaturation. LEFT THIRD mostly quiet darker road and coastal vegetation to support a white text overlay added later in HTML. Leave generous breathing room above and around car. No people or dangerous driving. Art direction: freedom to move, quiet confidence, Brazilian coast, sophisticated automotive magazine cover.

## Prompt da moto

Use case: ads-marketing. Photorealistic automotive lifestyle photograph for a premium Brazilian vehicle protection website. Landscape ratio 3:2. A tasteful modern matte graphite and black 300cc naked street motorcycle, generic and unbranded, parked on a coastal road turnout on the northeast coast of Brazil. Full bike shown in profile angled subtly toward viewer, facing left, dominating lower central frame. Ocean and green coastal hills behind, soft pale blue afternoon sky, weathered asphalt, golden late-afternoon rim light, restrained navy teal and natural warm stone color palette. No people. Real editorial photography, 50mm lens, beautiful natural reflections, authentic realistic wheels and mechanical components. Sophisticated, calm, free, outdoors, not a racing or stunt image. No text, no logos, no watermark, no graphical elements. Image must be a real-looking photo with wide breathing room around motorcycle.
